SCRAPLAB 1.1 - BUILD. BREAK. UNDERSTAND.

PLAY ON THIS COMPUTER
Double-click launch.cmd in this folder. No chat or internet is needed.
You can make a desktop shortcut to launch.cmd. Keep the original here.

MOVE THE ARM
1. In the workshop, choose Examples > Forklift.
2. Select its powered arm and choose Try in Practice Bay.
3. Hold E or Up to raise it. Hold Q or Down to lower it.
   You can click the visible buttons for a nudge, or hold them to move.
4. Hold A/D or Left/Right to drive. Space pauses/resumes; Esc edits.

The automatic benchmark does not accept manual controls. Choose Free Play.
Custom arms need a battery, an attached pivot, positive motor settings,
and the ARM E/Q assignment. A passive arm has no motor.
The arm status panel explains power, control, load and joint limits.

KEEP YOUR ROBOTS
The last draft recovers automatically. Ctrl+S saves a named blueprint;
Ctrl+O loads one. Your named files are in saves/. Export measurements
from the Lab Notebook before closing if you want to keep them.

NEW COMPUTER OR EXTRACTED ZIP
Install Python 3.11+ (tested on 3.14.7), run setup.cmd once with internet,
then launch.cmd. The ZIP contains source, not a portable Windows EXE.

README.md has all controls, setup, physics limits and project details.
docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md has a 60-90 second demonstration.
docs/VALIDATION.md records the checks actually performed.
